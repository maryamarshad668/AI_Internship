# Day 1 AI Learning

## What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of computers or machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making.

### Example
A voice assistant like Siri or Google Assistant.

---

## What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a branch of AI where computers learn patterns from data and make predictions without being explicitly programmed.

### Example
Netflix recommending movies based on your watching history.

---

## What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning (DL) is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with many layers to solve complex problems like image recognition and speech recognition.

### Example
Face recognition in smartphones.

---

## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of AI that creates new content such as text, images, music, or code based on what it has learned.

### Example
ChatGPT generating answers or DALL·E creating images from text prompts.
